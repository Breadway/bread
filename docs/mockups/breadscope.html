What is real here

The mockup is static except for the simulated stream. Every panel maps to a real IPC method.

Backed by existing IPC

  • events.subscribe and events.replay feed the stream and filters.
  • caused_by on each event builds the causal tree and inspector chain.
  • health gives version, uptime, adapters, subscriptions, and recent errors.
  • modules.list and state.dump give module status and store.
  • workflows.list, widgets.list, profile.list fill their views.
  • emit powers the dialog, including the reserved-domain rejection.

Needs a small IPC addition

  • Per-module subscriptions and handler latency are not exposed today. modules.list has no subscription list, and events carry no dispatch trace. The "Handled by" rows and the subscription counts on module cards assume a new method, for example subscriptions.list, and per-event handler timing.
  • Event volume history is not in the API either. The strip is simulated; a real implementation would keep a ring of counts client-side.

Deliberately mocked

  • The ticker injecting chains every 3.4 s, the latencies, and the 15 minute counts.
  • Widget previews render from a mock tree; real trees come from widgets.list.
